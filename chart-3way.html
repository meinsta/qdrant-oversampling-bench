Recall is scored against exact KNN on the same collection, so 1.000 is reachable by construction and nothing here is limited by the embedding model. Latency percentiles are the median of three passes of 1,000 sequential queries on local gRPC, roughly 0.3 ms of which is transport. Holding hnsw_ef at 300 keeps the graph walk identical across all six points: at these limits and 3× oversampling the candidate pool is 30 and 300, both at or below 300, so max(hnsw_ef, limit × oversampling) resolves to 300 everywhere.
