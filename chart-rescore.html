Qdrant 1.19.0 · 100k OpenAI vectors · 1,000 queries

Rescoring is the whole feature.
Oversampling cannot replace it.

Binary quantization throws away almost everything about a vector. Rescoring reads the originals back to re-rank what the compressed index found — and without it, no amount of oversampling helps, because there is nothing to re-rank with. Measured at limit=10, the most conservative of the four limits tested.

Recall is scored against exact KNN on the same collection, so 1.000 is reachable by construction and nothing here is limited by the embedding model. Both series run at the engine default hnsw_ef, which measures as 100. Latency percentiles are the median of three passes of 1,000 sequential queries each, on local gRPC. Method, raw results and the fuller report: meinsta/qdrant-oversampling-bench.
